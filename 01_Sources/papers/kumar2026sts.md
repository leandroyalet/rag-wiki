> **Summary** Comprehensive survey of Semantic Textual Similarity (STS) research post-2021, across six methodological streams: (1) transformer architectures (FarSSiBERT, DeBERTa-v3), (2) contrastive learning (AspectCSE), (3) domain-specific approaches (CXR-BERT for medical, Financial-STS), (4) multi-modal methods, (5) graph-based systems, (6) knowledge-enhanced techniques. Key finding: domain adaptation is consistently effective for specialized STS tasks. Directly relevant to the quality of embeddings used in RAG retrievers — embedding models trained or evaluated on STS benchmarks underpin [[Dense Retrieval]] and [[MTEB]] Retrieval scores.
