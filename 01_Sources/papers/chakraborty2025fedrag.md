> **Summary** Systematic mapping study (Kitchenham guidelines) of federated RAG literature 2020–2025. Federated RAG combines Federated Learning (distributed training, data stays local) with RAG (grounding LLM in external knowledge) for privacy-sensitive domains (healthcare, finance). Three major open challenges identified: privacy-preserving retrieval, cross-client heterogeneity, and evaluation limitations. New stub page created at [[02_Wiki/concepts/Federated RAG]].
