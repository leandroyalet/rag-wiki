> **Summary** Introduces MTEB, the standard leaderboard for text embedding models — 8 task types, 58 datasets, 112 languages, 33 models benchmarked. Key finding: no single embedding method dominates across all tasks. Retrieval task uses NDCG@10; evaluated on classification, clustering, pair classification, reranking, STS, summarisation, and bitext mining in addition to retrieval. Underpins the [[02_Wiki/benchmarks/MTEB]] wiki page.
