> **Summary** Proposes a reusable evaluation blueprint for RAG systems with three principles: (1) careful baseline and metric selection, (2) systematic refinements guided by qualitative failure analysis, (3) transparent reporting of design decisions for replication. Applied to software configuration dependency validation — achieves highest accuracy in that domain. Findings added as evaluation methodology note in [[02_Wiki/concepts/RAG Failure Points]].
